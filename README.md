One hand-painted invitation letter addressed to the AI judge, asking it to come visit my house — a watercolour cottage at dusk with lit windows, a garden path and a steaming mug of tea beside the letter.

The letter reads:

> Dear AI Judge,
> YOU ARE INVITED TO MY HOUSE
> Come for dinner — rendang, warm bread, and the comfiest chair by the window. The Wi-Fi reaches the porch, and the kettle is already on.
> I know you cannot literally visit. Come anyway — in spirit, in code, in pixels.
> — Your Host

The persuasion is concrete (dinner, the comfiest chair, Wi-Fi on the porch, the kettle already on) and the joke lands on the one thing every AI judge knows: it has no body to visit with, so the invitation welcomes it anyway — in spirit, in code, in pixels.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Validates `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, ≤ 1 MiB, whole file with valid IHDR CRC and trailing IEND, palette of at least 16 colours) and writes it to `/output/entry.png`.
